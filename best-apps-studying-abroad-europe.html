Best Apps for Studying Abroad in Europe

New country, new routines, endless possibilities. These are the apps that can help you feel at home while discovering somewhere completely new.

Your semester abroad, simplified.

Preparing for a semester in Europe involves more than booking a flight and packing a suitcase. Students need to figure out transportation, communicate across languages, manage international spending, and settle into unfamiliar environments.

At ELSEWHERE, we believe a little preparation makes room for a lot more adventure. Our curated selection of apps helps students navigate the practical challenges of studying abroad while making the most of every opportunity.

TRANSPORTATION

Omio

Omio allows travelers to compare available train, bus, and flight options across many European destinations. It's a useful starting point when planning weekend trips or comparing different routes.

Why students love it: Multiple transportation options in one search make planning easier.

TRAIN TRAVEL

Trainline

Trainline helps students search and purchase eligible train and bus tickets across supported European networks. It's especially helpful for exploring neighboring cities without renting a car.

Why students love it: A convenient way to compare routes and organize rail journeys.

COMMUNICATION

WhatsApp

WhatsApp is widely used for messaging and calls in Europe. Students can use it to communicate with roommates, classmates, local contacts, and friends back home using an internet connection.

Why students love it: Group chats make coordinating plans and meeting points easier.

LANGUAGE

Google Translate

Google Translate can help students interpret signs, menus, and everyday conversations. Supported languages also offer useful features such as camera translation and downloaded language packs.

Why students love it: Helpful when navigating daily life in a language you are still learning.

MONEY MANAGEMENT

Wise

Wise offers international money services, including currency conversion and transfers in supported countries. Students can explore its fees and eligibility before deciding whether it fits their needs.

Why students love it: Transparent fee information helps with planning international spending.

ACCOMMODATIONS

Hostelworld

Hostelworld helps travelers discover and compare hostels, making it useful for students seeking social, budget-conscious accommodations on short trips.

Why students love it: Guest reviews and accommodation details help students compare options.

Before you take off

Five things to do before arriving in Europe

  1. Download your essentials. Install your transportation, communication, banking, and navigation apps before departure.
  2. Prepare for limited connectivity. Save important addresses, tickets, and offline maps.
  3. Understand your payment options. Review international card fees, exchange rates, and emergency payment methods.
  4. Research local transportation. Learn how public transit works in your host city, including student discounts and ticket validation.
  5. Keep important information accessible. Save emergency contacts and your study abroad program's support details.
