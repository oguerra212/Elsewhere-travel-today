Best Travel Apps for Europe

From spontaneous flights to scenic train rides, Europe is yours to discover. Travel farther, plan smarter, and find your next favorite place.

Big adventures. Student-sized budgets.

One of the most exciting parts of studying abroad in Europe is how many destinations can become a weekend adventure. But comparing airlines, finding reliable train routes, and booking accommodations can quickly become complicated.

We've gathered useful travel apps to help you plan the journey, compare options, and navigate your destination with greater confidence. Prices and availability change, so always verify the details before booking.

Travel smarter, not harder

The weekend getaway checklist.

Planning an adventure shouldn't feel like another college assignment. Before you head out, use this quick checklist to keep the essentials organized.

  • Compare flights, trains, and buses before booking.
  • Check luggage rules and transportation fees.
  • Confirm passport, visa, and entry requirements.
  • Research where your accommodation is located.
  • Save tickets and reservation details offline.
  • Share your itinerary with someone you trust.
  • Leave extra time for transfers and delays.
  • Make room for unexpected discoveries.
