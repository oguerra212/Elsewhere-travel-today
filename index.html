The story behind ELSEWHERE

Made for the first-time explorers.

ELSEWHERE began with a familiar feeling: arriving in Europe for the first time, excited about everything ahead but completely unsure where to begin. Missed trains, expensive last-minute flights, confusing transportation systems, and unfamiliar apps can turn an incredible opportunity into a stressful experience.

Our fictional founder, Maya Ellis, imagined a space where students could find the resources she wished she had known about before studying abroad in Florence. ELSEWHERE combines practical travel guidance with a place to capture the experiences that make a semester unforgettable.

Our mission: To empower students studying abroad to navigate unfamiliar places with confidence, discover affordable adventures, and turn every journey into a story worth keeping.
